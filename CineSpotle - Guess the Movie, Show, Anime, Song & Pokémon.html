How to Play CineSpotle

Guess the mystery Movie, TV Series, Anime, Song, or Pokémon in 10 tries or fewer.

● Green Tiles indicate an exact attribute match with the mystery target.

● Amber Tiles indicate a close match (within ±5 years, ±20% Box Office/Popularity, ±100 Pokédex #, ±15 Base Stats, shared cast/type/move, or same Pokémon evolution line). Use ↑ or ↓ arrows to adjust.

Clues & Audio: Hints unlock from Guess 3 (max 3 per game, 1 per guess). OST Clips unlock at Guess 8 (Movies/TV/Anime) and Song Clips unlock at Guess 9.
